Why travelers trust Wayfarer over basic prompts

Most AI trip planners ping-pong across a city and treat hotel stays like daily attractions. We solved the 4 core pitfalls.

Neighborhood-First Clustering

Every day is organized strictly within a tight geographic radius, calculating actual walking & transit friction so you never waste hours commuting.

The Persistent Lodging Anchor

Accommodation is modeled as a persistent home base with morning departures and evening returns — completely solving the "Stay at Hotel A" flaw.

Live Weather-Driven Scheduling

16-day live weather forecasts directly shape activities: rainy mornings automatically shift to indoor museums, arcades, and sheltered markets.

Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Open industry standards allow you to export one-click RFC 5545 calendar feeds for Google/Apple Calendar and publication-grade vector PDF dossiers.

A pipeline you can trust

Five focused nodes. One clear flow. You sit in the loop.

1

Orchestrate

Validates your request and frames the task for the agents.

2

Research

Web search and live weather gather grounded context.

3

Plan

Drafts day-by-day with a transparent budget split.

4

Your gate

Approve, modify, or reject. Nothing finalizes without you.

5

Finalize

Locks in your approved itinerary, ready to go.

Meet the agents

Research Agent

Pulls LLM-ready context from the web, folds in live weather so rainy days become indoor days, and invokes MCP safety bulletins.

  • web_search
  • get_weather
  • MCP travel_safety

Planner Agent

Turns findings and budget into concrete days, complete with smart packing lists dynamically generated for your trip duration and interests.

  • allocate_budget
  • build_day_schedule
  • generate_packing_list

Human Gate

A real pause in the graph (LangGraph interrupt) that resumes exactly where it stopped — with your decision.

  • approve
  • modify
  • reject

Finalize Agent

Expands your approved draft into a rich itinerary, builds interactive route maps, and invokes MCP for visual PDF and calendar feeds.

  • build_route_map
  • MCP generate_pdf
  • MCP calendar_ics
