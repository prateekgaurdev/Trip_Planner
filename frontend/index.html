Meet the agents

Research Agent

Pulls LLM-ready context from the web, folds in live weather so rainy days become indoor days, and checks real-time exchange rates.

  • web_search
  • get_weather
  • get_exchange_rate

Planner Agent

Turns findings and budget into concrete days, complete with smart packing lists dynamically generated for your trip duration and interests.

  • allocate_budget
  • build_day_schedule
  • generate_packing_list

Human Gate

A real pause in the graph (LangGraph interrupt) that resumes exactly where it stopped — with your decision.

  • approve
  • modify
  • reject

Finalize Agent

Expands your approved draft into a rich itinerary, then geocodes every stop and computes walking/driving legs on an interactive map.

  • build_route_map
  • leg time estimates
  • total km traveled
